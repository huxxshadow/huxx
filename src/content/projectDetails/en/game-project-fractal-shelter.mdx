## About

**Fractal Shelter** is a game pitch for a **New Weird** Metroidvania in which comic panels are the basic units of space. The core idea is to make players feel they are *authoring the comic themselves*: they drag items onto map panels to change what happens in them, and explore a nonlinear canvas of panels with no single prescribed route.

The pitch covers the full concept from world and anomalies (FS-XXX) to core loop, art direction and a production timeline, and closes with an in-world recruitment flyer that extends the fiction beyond the document.
