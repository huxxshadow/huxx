## About

**Fractal Shelter** is a **New Weird** Metroidvania pitch that I co-created as a game designer for the USC 2027 Advanced Games Project (AGP) selection. Comic panels are the basic units of its space, and the core idea is to make players feel they are *authoring the comic themselves*: they drag items onto map panels to change what happens in them, and explore a nonlinear canvas of panels with no single prescribed route.

The pitch covers the full concept from world and anomalies (FS-XXX) to core loop, art direction and a production timeline, and closes with an in-world recruitment flyer that extends the fiction beyond the document.
