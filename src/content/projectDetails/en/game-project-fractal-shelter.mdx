## About

The game is built around comic panels as the fundamental units of space. You play Mr. Camera, a comic-book character pulled off the page into a collapsed underground facility overrun by anomalies (FS-XXX). By dragging items onto map panels to change their state and freely exploring a nonlinear canvas of panels, players gradually get the feeling of authoring the comic themselves.

I mainly worked on gameplay design, and served as narrative designer and art director.
