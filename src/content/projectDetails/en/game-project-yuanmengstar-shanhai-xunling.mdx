## My Role

During my internship at Tencent on *Yuanmengstar: Shanhai Xunling*, I participated as a **Combat Design Intern** in open-world gameplay development, primarily responsible for World Boss combat experience optimization and the design and advancement of new combat mechanisms. My specific responsibilities included:

* **World Boss "Cloud-Sea Qilin" — Design and Implementation**: Designed the World Boss fight, including its skills and attack rhythm, phase transitions, and how players are meant to respond; implemented the boss AI's decision-making and skill-casting logic with a **behavior tree**; and tuned and followed through on the **3C** (Character, Camera, Control) aspects of the fight — character control, camera behavior, skill responsiveness, target awareness, and combat feedback — to improve fluidity, readability, and overall feel.
* **New Combat Mechanic "Assist" — Designed and Driven from 0 to 1**: Served as the **PO (Gameplay Owner)** for "Assist", a new combat mechanic, responsible for the full process from system design, rule structuring, and flow construction through communication, integration verification, and implementation, driving the idea from concept to a system solution ready for integration into the live project.
* **Cross-Functional Communication and Collaboration**: Worked cross-functionally with design, art, and engineering colleagues during requirement advancement, participating in mechanism detail discussions, implementation alignment, issue tracking, and version iteration to ensure design goals were stably realized in the project environment.
* **Combat Experience Problem Analysis and Iterative Refinement**: Broke down existing experience issues based on actual development feedback and combat scenario requirements, produced targeted optimization recommendations, and continuously refined design details across multiple iterations to align the solution more closely with the project's overall pacing and player experience goals.
