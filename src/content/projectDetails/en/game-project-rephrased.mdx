## My Role

On the team I own engineering work on the 3C side, mainly implementing the camera zone blending system in C++ in Unreal Engine 5:

* **Weighted camera zone blending**: Each camera zone in a level gets a weight from how deep the player is inside it. Higher-priority zones claim their share first, equal priorities split theirs by weight, and the follow camera only gets what is left. Where two zones' transition bands overlap, the camera crosses straight from one zone to the other, with no hard cut and no dip back through the follow camera; location, rotation, field of view and aspect ratio all blend continuously by weight.
* **Transition bands and camera-jump fixes**: Zones now trigger through an outward transition band — the blend starts at the band's outer edge, completes at the zone core, and fades out symmetrically on exit. I also fixed a run of camera jumps: letterbox bars appearing in a single frame, hard cuts when entering a zone from above, handoff jumps when leaving the last zone, and wrong results on scaled zones.
* **Camera tutorial for designers**: Wrote the camera zone guide — how to place zones in the editor, which parameters to tune, the two fade modes, and a troubleshooting table — so designers can build level cameras on their own.
