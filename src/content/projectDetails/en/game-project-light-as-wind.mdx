## My Role

Five of us from USC Games teamed up for That Game Jam and made it in Unity 6 in two weeks. I directed it and also did the game design, lead programming and technical art:

* **Direction and design**: set the overall play and emotional arc, and strung ten levels into one journey.
* **Procedural creatures**: wrote every creature system in the game. The starfish, swordfish, crab and everything else in the sea have no keyframed animation; all their motion is generated by code in real time.
* **Pixel rendering**: built a render pipeline of my own for the 3D pixel-art look, replacing URP, so the pixels stay stable and don't flicker as the camera moves.
* **Tools**: made the level-editing tools and automated testing and recording tools, so five people could finish ten levels in two weeks.
