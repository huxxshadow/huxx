## My Role

### Camera

* **Layered FOV and dolly zoom**: Restructured the camera FOV into four stacked layers (base, speed, ability, hit feedback) and added a dolly zoom module that derives arm length from frustum width, keeping the character's size fixed while the background stretches.
* **Boost camera**: A state-driven fade layer drives FOV and dolly zoom together, so the lens opens and closes with the boost state; every parameter is data-driven and tunable in the Inspector.

### Drift

* **Drift movement**: Heading chases the target direction at a bounded turn rate to produce arcing paths; braking and charge accumulate per corner and pay out a speed burst on exit.
* **Drift camera**: FOV ramps open through a corner and pulses on exit, with camera yaw held inside a soft sector around the character's facing.

### Speed Shaders

* **SpeedLines**: Rewrote the noise-based Shader Graph as hand-written HLSL. The screen is split into angular slots with hashed selection, producing discrete, tapered lines (short head, long tail) that fade in and out; anti-aliasing is computed analytically to avoid a bright seam at the angle wrap.
* **RadialBlur**: A speed-driven full-screen post effect with two layers in one pass: a radial blur that keeps the center sharp and smears the edges (depth-gated so the horizon stays crisp), and a cartoon-style speed trail that stretches near objects backwards at high speed. The trail is a radial UV stretch inside discrete angular bands with depth tested at the source of the stretch, so tails spill past silhouettes and line up with the same spokes as the speed lines.
