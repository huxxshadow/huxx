## Setting

The game is set in an alternate 20th century, a world where the occult and mysterious powers are real.

After the First World War, humanity entered an age of unprecedented prosperity. Cars, radio, cinema, skyscrapers, consumer credit, jazz and speakeasies convinced everyone that they were standing at the threshold of a new era.

Beneath the surface world lies a primordial power known as **Follyblind**. And power comes at a price.

As the source of all things, Follyblind gives every aspect many facets, none of them simply good or evil; yet every power eventually drifts toward the extremity and blindness of its source.

Good or bad, anyone can overreach and walk into their own destruction.

> 'Tis the times' plague, when madmen lead the blind. — William Shakespeare, *King Lear*

Everything in the world is made of eight aspects: **Beast** (兽), **Fruit** (果), **Ember** (烬), **Molt** (蜕), **Sac** (囊), **Measure** (尺), **Forge** (铸) and **Key** (钥).
